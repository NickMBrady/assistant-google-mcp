Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Assistant Google MCP is a personal tool used only by its owner, Nicholas Brady, with their own Google accounts. It is not available to other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the tool accesses Gmail (read messages, create drafts; it never sends email), Google Calendar (read and edit events), and Google Drive (read-only).

How data is used

Data is retrieved only when the owner asks their AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic) to perform a task, and is used only to complete that task. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer.

Sharing

Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone other than the AI assistant processing the owner's own requests. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/NickMBrady/assistant-google-mcp/issues.
